## Payout patterns

Read from `src/data/paidDevelopmentPatterns.ts`, which is where their provenance
notes live. **Recorded and not yet used** — they are intended to replace the fitted
Weibull payout curves when the simplified model is built, and nothing reads them.

Age-to-ultimate factors, ground-up, ages in months from the START of the accident
year. GL is at a **limited layer whose limit was not supplied and must be added**
before wiring. WC's paid rows from 276 months are a **projected** tail, not measured.

⚠ **These are the patterns the game reads: the recorded ones compressed in time by Development speed**
(WC 1.5, GL 1.5, Property 1). The share paid and the share reported at age t
are the recorded shares at age speed x t, so the rows, ages and last age below are the compressed
ones. A row is marked projected or adjusted where a recorded row it reads was.

⚠ **No tail (Development tail).** Each paid pattern is then closed at its last row — the share paid at
each age divided by the share paid at the end — so nothing is outstanding there; the reported pattern
closes on paid's last step (the larger of the recorded share reported and the closed share paid).

along the closed reported pattern — the sum over development years k of the share newly reported in
year k x (1 + severity trend)^(k - 1). Each accident year's cost is booked with it.

A pool-layer WC pattern also exists and is deliberately not used — the game's
losses are ground-up. The GL "Total" paid pattern is retired for showing more paid
than reported at eight ages, 108 through 192 months. A second WC tail extension
jumping to ~9.3% emergence at 264 months was rejected for breaking the data's trend.

⚠ WC's incurred pattern is **measured to 264 months and projected from 276 to 444**,
continuing the last measured annual decline in the IBNR share (3.827%). Before this it
stopped at 264 while paid ran to 444, leaving the case/IBNR split undefined across the
gap; the split is now a derived presentation shown for every accident year, so the gap
had to close. All three lines' incurred patterns now end where their paid patterns do.
