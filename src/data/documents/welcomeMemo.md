**To:** Incoming Pool Management Team
**From:** Board of Governors
**Re:** Welcome to {{poolName}}

---

On behalf of the Board of Governors, welcome to {{poolName}}. You are taking responsibility for the Pool as it
enters the {{year}} policy year. Your decisions will shape its financial position, its members' costs, and its
ability to meet its obligations for years to come.

The Pool covers {{lines}} and closed last year with about {{surplus}} in surplus.

## What the Pool is for

The Pool exists to give its members dependable coverage at a reasonable and stable cost. That takes more than a
good result in any one year: rates must stay adequate, claims must be funded, and surplus must be strong enough to
carry the Pool through years that go worse than expected.

## How each year works

At the start of each year you will review the Pool's results and set its course for the year ahead: its funding,
its reinsurance, its underwriting, its investments, and any dividend or assessment. Then the year unfolds. New
claims arrive and older ones develop. Investments rise or fall. And events outside your control may arrive without
warning.

Each year's results become the starting point for the next — and many decisions show their full effect only later.

{{MULTI_LINE}}

## The Board's expectations

We do not expect you to eliminate risk. We do expect you to understand the risks you take, weigh their effect today
and years from now, and keep the Pool strong enough to withstand a difficult year. Success will not be measured by
any single year's surplus, but by how well you balance affordability, adequate funding, responsible growth and
long-term strength.

We wish you success.

**Board of Governors**
